Purpose & Vision

This Madrassa was established in 2021 with the purpose of providing quality Islamic education and strong moral training for female students. Our goal is to help young women grow in knowledge, character, and confidence, guided by the teachings of Islam.

We encourage our students to learn with sincerity, respect, and dedication. Along with Islamic knowledge, we also focus on good manners, responsibility, and preparing students to become positive members of society.

May Allah bless all our students, guide them on the right path, and accept their efforts.

Daily Curriculum

Our daily program (Taleem-ul-Baligha) covers four core disciplines ensuring a complete and balanced Islamic foundation for every student.

01
📖

Quranic Translation

ترجمۂ قرآن

Daily translation of selected verses with clear meanings and easy-to-understand explanations.

02
🌿

Tafseer-ul-Quran

تفسیرِ قرآن

Detailed explanation of verses, Shan-e-Nuzool (context of revelation), and key lessons.

03
📜

Nazira-e-Quran

نظرۂ قرآن

Daily recitation practice focusing on fluency and correct pronunciation of the Holy Quran.

04
🎵

Tajweed

تجوید

Makharij al-Huroof, rules of Ghunna, Madd, Waqf and daily recitation practice with proper articulation.

درسِ نظامی

6-Year Alimah Course

سال اول تا دوم: عربی زبان کی بنیاد (نحو و صرف)، بنیادی دینی مضامین، فقہ اور منطق
سال سوم تا چہارم: اعلیٰ عربی گرامر، اصولِ فقہ، تفسیر القرآن، علمِ العقائد
سال پنجم: فقہ، تفصیل کے ساتھ تفسیر، احادیثِ مبارکہ کا مطالعہ
سال ششم: صحاحِ ستہ – صحیح بخاری، صحیح مسلم، سنن ابی داؤد، جامع ترمذی

روزانہ نصاب

Daily Curriculum (Taleem-ul-Baligha)
